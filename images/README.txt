SOVELLE — PRODUCT PHOTOS
========================

Drop your product photos into THIS "images" folder using the exact
filenames below. The catalogue loads them automatically. Any photo
that is missing simply shows an emoji placeholder instead — so the
page always works even before all images are added.

Tips:
- Square photos (1:1) look best. JPG or PNG are both fine (keep the .jpg name).
- Keep each photo small (ideally under ~150 KB) so the page stays light
  and shares fast over WhatsApp.
